What are you making?

Picking one opens a conversation, not a form. You describe it, the machine asks a few questions and writes the first truth files as you answer, and nothing lands on disk until you say so. Grouped by what each form needs from you, because that is the question you are really answering.

From nothing You bring the idea

Not sure which

Describe it in a sentence and Quire will pick the form, set up the folder and write the first truth file. You can change the form afterwards.

Where this lives. In the running app these thirteen are a START CREATING list in the sidebar. Thirteen rail items would drown the four places you go every day, so the rail carries one door (Start something, top of the rail on every screen) and the choosing happens here, where each form has room to say what it needs from you. Synthetic content: the descriptions are authored demonstration data.
