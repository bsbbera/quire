The frame, and what it always tells you

Nothing on this screen is a page. It is the chrome every other screen sits inside, drawn once so that twenty-five screens cannot drift from each other.

Rail

196px, putty-2, collapsible to a 64px icon rail with the width remembered. Four production sections, then the machine: Setup, Images, Worlds. The active item grows a 3px vermilion bar from its centre rather than filling a box.

Topbar

Breadcrumbs left, status right. The model and shim pills are quiet because they are almost always fine. The waiting pill is the one loud object in the chrome, and it is the app heartbeat: the count of gates that need a person.
