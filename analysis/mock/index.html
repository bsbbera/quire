Synthetic content. Every book, issue, chapter, model name and percentage across these screens is authored demonstration data, written to exercise the layouts at real length. Nothing here is a claim about what the product does; the capabilities drawn are the ones PRODUCT.md and the redesign plan already describe.

The app is chat-first, and these screens now say so. ChatPage has four modes, and App.tsx routes book-create, chat, book and interactive-film-authoring all into it. Opening a book does not open a book page — it opens a conversation about that book. Picking a form does not open a form — it opens a conversation seeded for it. Screens 04 and 29 were rebuilt on that finding; the redesign plan draws both as static pages, and the plan is wrong about it.

Two corrections to the plan, both deliberate. The interface face is Geist, not Archivo: PRODUCT.md lists Geist as a pinned brand commitment, and a commitment outranks a plan document. And Audit follows plan §5 — findings on paper, the passage on charcoal — rather than audit-redesign.html, because Part I reserves charcoal for the work itself and a manuscript passage is exactly that.

The rail is the real one now. It used to carry an invented “Machine” group. The shipped sidebar has a System section (Genres, Model config, Project settings, Daemon, Logs) and a Tools section (Translation, Style, Import, Radar, Doctor, MCP, Setup), which together outrun any laptop — so the rail scrolls between a fixed wordmark and a pinned run pill. Three items carry a small grey dot: Images, Library and Worlds are in the redesign plan and have no route in the app. They are drawn, not shipped, and the rail says so rather than pretending.

Every route is now drawn. All twenty-eight pages the app actually routes have a screen here. Four of them were checked against App.tsx before drawing rather than after: film-author is a fourth ChatPage mode and is drawn as chat, while play, film and film-studio render StoryPlayer, StoryGraphTree and FilmWizard and are not conversations. Guessing that one wrong is what cost the rebuild of screen 04.
