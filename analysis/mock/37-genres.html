Rules

Handed to the writer verbatim, every chapter

Nothing supernatural is ever confirmed. The reader may believe it. A character may believe it. The book never does.

Violence happens off the page or after the section break. What is on the page is the aftermath and who has to clean it.

Every chapter ends on an ordinary domestic act — a kettle, a door, a light. Never on a revelation.

No dream sequences. No mirrors. No character describing their own face.
