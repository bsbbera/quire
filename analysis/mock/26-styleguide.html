Geometry · the identity

Discs, cropped by their panel

Zero to two per panel, at most one filled. They anchor to corners and bleed a quarter to a half out of frame. They are decorative, and they are never under text.

Halftone is reserved for the print side (magazine, images, layout), where the texture quietly means ink on paper.

Icons · one grid, one weight

Drawn on one 24px grid at 1.6 stroke, round caps and joins, no fills. Emoji and Unicode glyphs are not an icon system, and a set assembled from three sources reads as three sets. The mark itself is three nested arcs. A quire is the gathering of folded sheets that makes one signature of a book.

Motion · one easing, three durations

DurationUsed forWhy
150mshover, press, colourFast enough that it reads as the control responding rather than as an animation.
300msmovement, layout, rowsAnything that changes where something is.
640msfirst-run reveals onlyThe one screen someone reads top to bottom once. Never on a work screen.
cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)everythingOne easing. Anything outside this set is a bug, not a decision.

Motion conveys state and nothing else: a run advancing, a gate clearing, an approval leaving the list. Under prefers-reduced-motion every sequence jumps to its final state. Nothing animates within reach of a reading column.
