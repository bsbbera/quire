Approve the board Six sections, four worlds locked. Approving writes the flatplan and starts the copy for all 64 pages.

Stand-in artwork. The world plates are labelled placeholders, not renders — a mockup ships no assets. In the app each plate is the section’s sample spread, cropped by a disc. Synthetic content: sections, page titles and worlds are authored demonstration data.
