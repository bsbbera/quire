One thing to know

Cut paper and hand lettering pull against each other. Cut paper has no drawn line in it, so lettering reads as a sticker on top of the artwork rather than part of it. Nothing stops you; the sample above is what it looks like.

Stand-in artwork. The sample page is a labelled placeholder; in the app it is a real render, rebuilt on every change. Synthetic content: the systems, techniques, props and the compatibility warning are authored demonstration data.
