What the discs mean

The silhouette is the type. It is drawn once here and never labelled again anywhere else in the app.

SilhouetteTypeWhy
BookThe filled disc. The default, and the heaviest mark, because a novel is the heaviest thing on the shelf.
MagazineHalftone. The texture is what ink on paper looks like up close, so it means print without saying so.
StorybookA pair of rings — two facing pages, which is the unit a storybook is actually written in.
ShortThe same mark, smaller. One sitting.
ScriptTwo rules, not a disc. A script is ruled paper and speaker names, and a circle would be a lie about its shape.

Synthetic content. Titles, word counts and chapter positions are authored demonstration data.
